Careers

A useful trade, worksites that matter.

Pipelayers, site managers, plant operators, rehabilitation technicians: SA LA GARONNE recruits field professionals, entry-level and experienced, to build and maintain the water networks of Toulouse.

Team 35 employeesCrews 4 site crewsPositions Based in ToulouseJob application Speculative welcome

Why join us

A human-sized company, and no two worksites alike.

At SA LA GARONNE, no worksite is anonymous. Every week our crews work on networks the city relies on daily, often in conditions few companies take on: city centre, deep excavation, live networks.

We are 35 people, split across four site crews, a workshop and a design office. Three generations of the same family have led the company since 1956: we know each other, we talk, and decisions are made quickly.

We recruit regularly, to strengthen our crews and to pass on know-how that can only be learned by doing.

What we offer

Six good reasons to join us.

A stable workplace, modern methods and safety that is never negotiable.

01A job with meaning

Drinking water in, wastewater out: our worksites serve everyone, every day.

02A family business

35 employees, an approachable management, stable crews where everyone is known by their first name.

03Safety first

A dedicated QHSE manager, AIPR, CATEC, first aid and H0B0 training, equipment supplied and checked.

04Modern methods

Trenchless rehabilitation, robotic milling, the in-house RIC inspection robot: you learn the techniques of tomorrow.

05Passing on skills, moving up

Mentoring between generations of pipelayers and site managers, regular training, internal progression.

06Concrete commitments

A labelled CSR programme, local fruit and vegetable baskets offered every month, a CSR committee open to employees.

The roles

The profiles we recruit.

Field and design office positions, permanent, apprenticeship or internship depending on the year's needs.

Role
Pipelayer

Laying sewer and drinking water pipes, service connections, manholes, reinstatement. The heart of our trade, as a crew, on site.

ProfileMotivated beginner or experienced; driving licence appreciated.
Role
Site manager

Site organisation, safety, phasing, liaison with the client and residents, crew supervision.

ProfileExperience in pipelaying or civil engineering, organisational skills.
Role
Plant operator

Earthworks, lifting, laying in shored trenches, including deep excavations and city-centre sites.

ProfileValid plant licence, urban experience appreciated.
Role
Trenchless rehabilitation technician

CIPP lining, robotic milling, CCTV inspection, operating the mobile units.

ProfileTraining provided in-house; a taste for technology and precision.
Role
Design office: surveyor-draughtsman, design engineer

Surveys, construction and as-built drawings, cost studies, support for the site crews.

ProfileSurveying or civil engineering background, command of drafting tools.
Role
Apprenticeships and internships

We welcome apprentices and interns on site and in the design office.

ProfileVocational, technical or engineering studies: tell us about your plans.

The process

How it works.

Four simple steps, and a reply to every application.

Stage 01Job application

Send your CV and a few lines about your background, via the form or by email. We reply to every application.

Stage 02Phone call

A first conversation to understand your experience and expectations, and to present the role.

Stage 03Meeting at head office

An interview in Toulouse, with a tour of the workshop and, when possible, of a live worksite.

Stage 04Onboarding

Safety induction, equipment provided, paired with an experienced colleague for the first weeks.
